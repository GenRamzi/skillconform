# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Examples of positive behavior include constructive feedback, empathy, clear technical disagreement, and giving credit for others' work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces, and public interactions representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a repository Security Advisory. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately and fairly as possible. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
